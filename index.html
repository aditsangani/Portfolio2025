★ HACKABULL VII · 1ST PLACE OF 200

PostureGuard.

Better posture, without sending your camera feed anywhere.

A Chrome and Edge extension built with a four-person team. It uses MediaPipe pose estimation to catch “tech neck” in real time, then coaches you back with alerts, streaks, and a short reset routine.

ReactViteTailwind CSSMediaPipe
Devpost ↗ Code ↗

WHY IT WON

1st of 200 participants

First place overall at HackaBull VII, April 2026.

0 frames leave your device

All inference runs locally in the browser, with a personalized baseline calibration.

How it works +

MediaPipe returns landmarks for the body; PostureGuard uses the ear, shoulder, and hip points and a custom angle calculation to measure how far your head drifts forward. A short calibration sets your personal baseline, so alerts fit your body instead of a generic threshold.

After extended poor posture it nudges you with a notification and a guided reset. A dashboard tracks hourly posture scores and fatigue patterns, and streaks and achievements keep the habit going.
